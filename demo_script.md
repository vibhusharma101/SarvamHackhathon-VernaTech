# Demo Script — ~2 minutes

**Setup before you start:** candidate tab open at `/candidate` (not yet started), recruiter console open on a second screen/tab, one paired harness result already computed and visible on `/harness`.

---

### 0:00–0:15 — The hook

> "Technical interviews score how well someone speaks English, not how well they engineer. If you're not a native English speaker, that's a tax on your interview score that has nothing to do with your skill. We built a screening tool that scores the engineering — and proves, on camera, that it's not scoring the language."

---

### 0:15–0:45 — Live candidate flow

*(Open `/candidate`, type a name, check the consent box)*

> "Candidate enters their name, consents to being recorded — and that's it, no language picker."

*(Click "Begin technical screen." Hold the mic button, answer the first question in Hindi or Hinglish — e.g. the production-debugging one. Release.)*

> "They just answered entirely in Hindi. Watch the corner —"

*(Point at the language-detected toast)*

> "— it detected the language live, per answer, no setup needed."

---

### 0:45–1:15 — Recruiter console reveal

*(Switch to the console, select this session)*

> "On the recruiter side: here's the score — 4 out of 5 — and here's *why*."

*(Point at the quoted evidence blockquote under the criterion)*

> "Every score is a quote, not a vibe. And over here —"

*(Point at the right-hand transcript panel)*

> "— original Hindi, next to the English gloss the scorer actually read. Full traceability, nothing hidden."

---

### 1:15–1:40 — The memory feature

> "Say the recruiter learns something new after the interview — the candidate clarified something in a follow-up chat. We don't throw that away."

*(Type a note into one criterion's note box — e.g. "candidate later confirmed they added monitoring to prevent recurrence" — hit Save & Rescore)*

> "That note is tagged to this exact criterion. Rescore —"

*(Wait for the score to update)*

> "— and it's incorporated immediately, and it'll be remembered on every future rescore too. This isn't a static score, it's a living one."

---

### 1:40–2:00 — The fairness proof, and close

*(Switch to `/harness`)*

> "And here's the actual proof: the same content, answered once in English, once in the candidate's own language. The gap between them — measured, not assumed. When it's near zero, that's the system working. When it's not, we don't hide it — we flag it."

*(Point at a low-MAD pair, or the disclosed-bias warning if one is flagged)*

> "Score the engineering. Not the English."

---

## Cut-for-time notes
- If running short on time, skip the memory/rescore beat (1:15–1:40) and go straight from console to harness — the two strongest beats are the live-language answer and the fairness harness gap.
- If the STT is slow on the day, have a second session already fully answered and scored as a fallback to jump straight to the console reveal.
