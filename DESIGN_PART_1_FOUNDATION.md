# DESIGN.md — Part I: Foundation

## Vision
Build the world's most trustworthy AI technical interview platform.

### Product Thesis
Engineering claims should be evaluated by evidence, not fluency.

## Design Principles
1. Evidence over opinions.
2. Editorial over dashboard.
3. Calm over flashy.
4. Research over marketing.
5. Explainable over magical.

## Brand Keywords
- Trustworthy
- Technical
- Editorial
- Minimal
- Auditable
- Transparent

## Visual DNA
- Warm paper background
- Editorial serif headings
- Sans UI
- Monochrome with restrained teal accent
- Flat surfaces
- Quote-first interfaces

## Core User Journey
Speech → Transcript → Intent → Evidence → Rubric → Verdict → Audit Trail

## Design Goals
- Recruiters understand *why* a verdict was produced.
- Candidates can inspect supporting evidence.
- Every score is traceable to transcript spans.
