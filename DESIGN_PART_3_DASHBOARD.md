# DESIGN.md — Part III: Dashboard

## Philosophy
The dashboard is an evidence review workspace, not an analytics dashboard.

## Information Architecture
- Interviews
- Candidates
- Rubrics
- Research
- Team
- Settings

## Candidate Review
### Left
- Transcript
- Search
- Speaker timeline

### Center
- Evidence highlights
- Intent extraction
- Rubric mapping

### Right
- Verdict
- Category scores
- Supporting quotes
- Audit trail

## Rules
- Never show a score without evidence.
- Clicking a verdict scrolls to transcript evidence.
- Hovering transcript highlights linked rubric items.

## Empty States
- No interviews
- Processing
- Failed transcription
